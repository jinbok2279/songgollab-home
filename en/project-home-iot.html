Songgol Home IoT · AI wall pad

An AI wall pad tailored to the house without rewiring. I bring device installation, a floor-plan screen and remote access together. I am testing it upstairs at our house and at La Grills, a self-service BBQ site.

Email meHome IoT explained
FeaturesOverviewIn daily useDevelopment logVersion history
Features

The whole house on one screen

Home and roomsLarge cards show time, weather, family presence, lights and room temperatures.
3D floor planA floor plan follows the shape of our house. Tap a room to see its devices and lights.
Family viewSee family members’ phone locations and battery levels with their consent.
EnergySee measured appliance energy use and estimated costs.
AutomationDevices respond to the conditions at home. Features without the required sensors are not shown as working.
Voice assistantAsk by voice and hear the answer on the tablet or a connected speaker.
AppliancesSee connected appliance status and receive notices such as the end of a wash.
Device checksCheck the health of registered devices at a glance.
Shared settingsThe laptop and tablet share the same settings.

Development log

How it got here

  • 2026.10.02First plan for our houseThe aim was simple: see the house on one screen, reduce wasted electricity and keep costs down. I started upstairs without electrical work.
  • 2026.10.05An upstairs floor planAdded a 3D view of upstairs, with room-level lights and devices.
  • v1.0 ~ v1.2First connected home viewAdded lights, family view and weather, then improved tablet access and 3D performance.
  • v1.3 ~ v1.5Speaker and voice assistantAdded a speaker, voice interaction and robot-vacuum controls.
  • v1.6 ~ v1.7Appliances and energy useAdded appliance status, energy use, estimated costs and end-of-wash alerts.
  • v1.8 ~ v1.9Fixing problems as they appearedFixed connection and display issues, then improved the microphone and family map.
  • 2026.10.04A second site — La GrillsTook the home project into a working business for the first time with La Grills Hub v2.6.
  • v1.10Shared settings and device checksShared settings across the laptop and tablet, added device checks and made unsupported functions clear.
  • 2026.10.11Five Home IoT videosPublished five short videos about IoT and its everyday benefits. Home IoT explained →
  • NextMore real-world automationPlans include room-based presence and temperature controls, with lighting, booking schedules and energy monitoring at La Grills.
