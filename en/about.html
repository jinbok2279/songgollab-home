About Songgol Lab

Built by hand,
used for real, then proven

Songgol Lab started building in October 2026. I use what I make at home and at our shop, fix it as I go, and only polish into products the things that prove useful.

Name and symbol

Songgol, the name of my home village

Songgol is the village I come from. I started building home-IoT screens there, and the symbol interlocks ㅅ and ㄱ, the first letters of Song and Gol in Korean.

How I work

Technology proves itself in real use

1We use it firstEverything runs in our own house and shop first, and gets fixed before it goes public.
2We show it as it isReal scope and real development stage, not inflated numbers.
3We keep refiningFeatures and screens keep improving from what users tell us.
