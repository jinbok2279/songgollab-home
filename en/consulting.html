Semiconductor · Wet Clean Process & Equipment

Semiconductor process and equipment engineering

For 20 years I investigated defects on semiconductor wet-clean equipment, looking at software, hardware, sequences and controls together. This page records that experience.

See my experience →
Experience

The symptoms were visible,
but the cause was not

Equipment problems often involve several things at once. I learned to look at software, hardware, operating sequences and controls together.

  • Intermittent particles or splashing with an unclear cause
  • Inconsistent results after process changes
  • Waiting and bottlenecks that reduce throughput
  • Plenty of data without a useful way to review it
  • Repetitive manual evaluations that take too long

My approach

Evidence I could reproduce,
rather than guesswork

A cause needs evidence that can be reproduced under the same conditions.

Review the evidenceLogs and history
