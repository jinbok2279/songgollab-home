Drive Console development log

A spare tablet becomes a dashboard, navigation screen and vehicle-information display. I brought together a 3D dashboard, TMAP split screen, radio, voice assistant and OBD readings, and checked the connection and fault-code reading in my 2017 Kia K7 3.0 LPi.

Development log

How it got here

  • v0.6First dashboard frameStarted with a v0.6 installer and added screens and features one at a time.
  • v0.9Dashboard and split screenAdded dashboard designs, split-screen controls and six languages. Removed placeholder readings.
  • v1.0Wi-Fi OBD connectionAdded live vehicle readings and estimated fuel-use information.
  • v1.0.1 ~ v1.0.11Split-screen and voice refinementsRefined TMAP split screen, voice interaction, weather, backgrounds and floating-window support over eleven updates.
  • v1.1Bluetooth and vehicle informationAdded Bluetooth OBD support and a vehicle-information screen with readings and explanations.
  • v1.2Home screen and internet radioAdded the vehicle-status panel, 3D vehicle and eight radio channels, with playback across screens.
  • v1.2.1Broader fault-code explanationsAdded explanations for 427 Kia K7 and 9,415 standard codes, plus readings from other supported systems.
  • v1.2.2 ~ v1.2.3Driving-safety refinementsAdded the driving video cover and refined screen transitions and the voice overlay. The recorded button test found no errors.
  • v1.3Petrol, diesel and LPiAdded fuel-specific information and reviewed the explanatory text.
  • v1.3.1Returning from split screenFixed the return to the home screen after closing split screen.
  • v1.3.53Custom assistant name and driving noticeAdded a custom assistant name and a driving-safety notice on first launch.
  • NextMore vehicle testing, then releasePlans include more real-vehicle checks, decisions about radio, a certified-dongle package and store release preparation.
