WHY I BUILT IT

A fault code alone was never enough

I have used OBD tools for a long time, but the apps only showed a code number, so I had to search for its meaning every time.

So I built an app that explains the cause and what to check the moment a code appears. I use it in my own car and keep improving it.

What it does

01

Live gauges

Speed, RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage and fuel economy in real time.

02

Fault codes, explained

Reads DTCs and tells you what they mean and what to check, including Hyundai and Kia codes.

03

Split screen

Navigation and YouTube side by side. Video apps are hidden automatically while driving.

04

Voice assistant

Say "Songgollab, play YouTube". You can change the wake name.

05

Car log & trips

Keep expenses and trip records on your device.

06

Music & photos

Pick a folder and play your MP3s and photos.

SETUP

Five steps

  1. 01

    Plug in the dongle

    Insert it into the OBD port under the dashboard. A light means it's ready.

  2. 02

    Mount the tablet

    Stick the mount to the dashboard and place the tablet.

  3. 03

    Install the app

    Apply as a tester and you'll get an install link: tap 'Become a tester' → 'Download' on Google Play.

  4. 04

    First-run setup

    Only 'Display over other apps' is required. Microphone and location are optional.

  5. 05

    Connect the dongle

    Choose your Bluetooth OBD dongle in Settings › Connection. The engine must be on.

FAQ

How do I install it?

Once you're registered as a tester you'll receive a link. Open it, tap 'Become a tester' → 'Download', and it installs from Google Play.

The dongle won't connect

Turn the engine on, enable Bluetooth on the tablet and pick the dongle again in Settings › Connection. Close any other app that is using the dongle.

Some values are grey

Each car reports different values. Grey means a reference value without a standard.

Can I use it while driving?

Operate it while stopped and use the voice assistant while driving. Video apps are hidden automatically on the move.

Can I test without a car or dongle?

Yes, you can still join as a tester.
