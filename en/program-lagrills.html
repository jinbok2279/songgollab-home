La Grills Hub

I control the air conditioners across BBQ sites 1–9, guest rooms and the shop at La Grills from one counter PC. This was the first project I took from our home into a working business.

Running on siteWindowsv2.6IoT · Automation
FeaturesHow it worksVideoResourcesDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

Before guests arrived, someone had to walk around with a remote and switch on each zone. After checkout, someone had to check again. It was easy to miss one during busy hours.

ONE LINE

Turns zone-by-zone air conditioners at a self-service BBQ place on and off from one counter PC.

Features

What it does

Each zone on one screenSwitch the air conditioning for BBQ sites, guest rooms and the shop on or off by zone.
Keep the existing equipmentThe air conditioners stay in place; the hub brings their controls together.
Always at the counterThe screen stays open on the shop PC so I can see which zones are on.
Two days, no rewiringInstalled without new wiring on October 4, 2026.
