Butler Robot

I am turning a broken robot vacuum and an unused 3D printer into a home robot that comes when called and carries water. The target for new parts is about KRW 100,000. Design is complete; measurements have just started.

Design doneRobot vacuum + 3D printer partsStage 0IoT · Automation

Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

A worn wheel gear stopped a Samsung Jet Bot AI, but much of the machine was still usable. An old 3D printer was also sitting idle. I wanted to reuse them for a robot that could fit into our home IoT setup.

Features

What it does

Comes when calledThe goal is to travel to the person calling it.
Carries a cupA tray is planned for small deliveries such as water.
A tablet faceA tablet provides the face and interaction screen.
Adjustable heightThe design stays low while moving and raises the screen when stopped.
Reused vacuum baseKeeps useful parts from the broken robot vacuum.
Reused printer partsUses parts from an old printer to reduce new purchases.

Safety

Design priorities

Clear sensingKeep the surroundings visible to the robot.
Stable movementKeep the moving body low.
BalanceCheck stability before raising the screen.
Safe stoppingPlan for lost connections, obstacles and drops.
Covered moving partsProtect hot and moving components.
Check before spendingInspect critical donor parts before buying more.
Requirements and setup · New-parts target: about KRW 90,000–100,000, reduced from the first estimate by reusing available parts. Purchases follow checks at each stage. An existing home PC and tablet are planned for use.
