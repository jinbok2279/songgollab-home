Project Spend Tracker

A Windows program that organises AliExpress and Gmarket purchases by project and shows monthly spending at a glance. I mainly review the entries that need a decision.

In useWindowsv2.0Ops · AI tools
FeaturesHow it worksGalleryDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

With several DIY and IoT projects running together, I stopped remembering which project a part belonged to or how much I had spent. I wanted to see the totals without copying every order into a spreadsheet.

Features

What it does

DashboardFilter by month, project and shop. Correct a project directly in the purchase list.
Analysis in plain wordsSee spending trends, month-end estimates and accumulated costs.
Project budgetsCompare spending with a monthly budget and review large purchases.
Regular updatesKeep the purchase list current without duplicate or cancelled orders.
Central ControlSee totals, entries needing review and budget warnings on the board.
For makersDesigned around several DIY projects and frequent parts purchases.
