Social Media Hub

I manage Songgol Lab’s Instagram, Threads and blog in one program: writing, scheduling, checking reactions and finding useful topics.

InternalWindowsv0.6Ops · AI tools
FeaturesHow it worksGalleryDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

Opening each channel separately to post and check its numbers was taking too much time.

ONE LINE

Instagram, Threads and the blog in one program — write, schedule and track reactions.

Features

What it does

Channel overviewSee visits and reactions in figures and charts.
Write and scheduleAdapt a post for each channel and schedule it.
Photo postsPrepare posts from my photos.
Publishing queueKeep planned posts in order.
Popular postsUse the response to earlier posts when choosing the next topic.
Central ControlSee channel status on the main board.
