Home IoT explained

IoT can make the house take care of small everyday tasks. These five Home IoT stories videos explain what it means and what changes at home.

What is IoT?Five everyday benefitsA day in a smart homeReducing wasted powerChecking on parentsHow I approach it
EP.1

What is IoT?

IoT, the Internet of Things, connects household devices so they can sense, notify and act . Here is a simple four-part overview.

1SenseNotice changes such as temperature, movement or an open door.
2ConnectBring those changes into the home view.
3DecideDecide whether a response is needed.
4ActLights, air conditioning and plugs respond.

Together, these features let you see and manage the house from a phone while away.

EP.2

What changes at home?

Five ways IoT can help in everyday life.

ConvenienceUse a voice request or a button to control lights and air conditioning.
SavingsReduce lighting, cooling and standby power in unused rooms.
SafetyReceive a phone alert about an open door or something unusual.
Remote accessCheck the house and manage devices while away.
AutomationLet the house respond to routines such as arriving, leaving and bedtime.
EP.3

A day in a smart home

A few examples, from waking up to going to bed.

  • 07:00 · Wake upStart the dayCheck the house on one phone screen.
  • Going outTurn things off togetherSwitch off the lights and air conditioning together.
  • During the dayAn alert from homeReceive an alert if a door opens or movement is detected while away.
  • On the way homePrepare the roomStart heating or cooling before you arrive.
  • 23:00 · BedtimeLights off automaticallyLet the remaining lights turn off at bedtime.
EP.4

Reducing the power you did not know was on

Standby devices and lights in empty rooms can use power unnoticed. IoT helps make that use visible and reduce it.

See energy useSee appliance energy use and spot unnecessary consumption.
Reduce wasteTurn off empty-room lights and unused devices automatically.

I will report actual savings in the work log after collecting seasonal data from our test sites.

EP.5

Looking after parents from afar

A few sensors and alerts can help families check on a parent’s home from a distance.

Smoke and leaksReceive an alert when a connected sensor detects smoke or a leak.
DoorsCheck whether the front door is open or closed.
TemperatureSee whether the house is getting too cold or too hot.
Changes in routineNotice unusual changes in activity.
Remote heatingHelp warm the house remotely on a cold day.
