Central Control (HUD)

Central Control (SONGGOLLAB HUD) brings my home, work and programs onto one screen. Connected programs appear as cards around an AI core, and I can ask questions or give commands by voice.

In daily useWindows · Web · Phonev1.19.8Ops · AI tools
FeaturesHow it worksGalleryVideoResourcesDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

Home IoT, trading experiments, social channels, 3D printing and AI tools each had their own window. I wanted one place to see what was running, what had stopped and what needed attention.

Features

What it does

A card for each programConnection status and one key figure stay visible. Open a card for more detail.
Connected programs togetherNewly connected programs appear on the board; I can hide cards I do not need.
Voice or textAsk about a program or give a command without switching windows.
Confirm important actionsActions such as starting or stopping a print ask for confirmation.
Project overviewSee development stages, sign-in issues, expiry dates and subscriptions.
Phone and full screenUse the same board on a phone or show it full screen on a monitor.
