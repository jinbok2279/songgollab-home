Ali Sourcing Helper

Ali Sourcing Helper is a Windows program for reviewing overseas product candidates and preparing listing drafts for Naver Smart Store, including names, prices and introductory posts.

In developmentWindowsv2.1Commerce · Trading R&D
FeaturesHow it worksGalleryDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

Finding a product, editing its description, working out a price and writing a post meant repeating the same preparation each time.

Features

What it does

Product reviewCompare candidate products, prices, shipping and reviews.
Eligibility reviewFlag items that need attention before a listing is prepared.
Listing namesDraft clearer product names.
Margin viewReview costs and estimated margin.
CategoriesPrepare a suitable listing category.
Introductory postsDraft a blog introduction for a product.
