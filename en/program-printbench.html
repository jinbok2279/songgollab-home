3D Print Bench

A Windows program I built to watch and manage my 3D printer from one screen. It brings together print status, queued jobs, slicing, filament tracking and checks, on the home network without a cloud service.

In developmentWindowsv0.9IoT · Automation
FeaturesHow it worksSafetyGalleryVideoResourcesDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

A print can take hours. I kept walking to the printer or switching between its screen and the slicer. I started this program the day I brought home a used printer.

Features

What it does

Live monitoringSee progress, time remaining, estimated completion and temperature graphs, with a 3D view of the print path.
Queue and slicingQueue files and prepare models for printing in one place.
Preview before printingReview the layout, sliced result, first layer and layer paths.
Printers and filamentManage more than one printer and keep track of filament remaining.
Checks and calibrationBring routine checks and calibration into one screen.
Quiet nights and idle powerUse a quieter night mode and turn off idle equipment. View a connected camera.
How it works

How I use it

Safety

Checks before a print starts

MaterialWarns about a mismatch between the file and loaded filament.
TemperatureChecks whether the planned temperatures suit the material.
Printer and nozzleChecks printer and nozzle compatibility.
Filament remainingWarns if the roll may not have enough material.
Bed edgesFlags first-layer paths near the bed boundary.
A clear reasonExplains why a start was blocked and records any confirmed override.
Requirements and setup · Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Tested with a Creality K1 on stock firmware. Support for other printers is in preparation. The PC and printer use the same home network; automatic slicing requires OrcaSlicer.
