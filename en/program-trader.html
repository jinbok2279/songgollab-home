Songgol Trader

A private Windows project that brings my stock and cryptocurrency experiments onto one screen. It combines views for Kiwoom simulation, Toss stocks and Upbit crypto. My own private tool — not for sale.

In developmentWindowsv0.3.0Commerce · Trading R&D
FeaturesHow it worksSafetyGalleryVideoResourcesDevelopment log
Why I built it

It started with a small annoyance

Separate stock and crypto programs meant two sets of windows and reviews. I brought the views together so I could follow them in one place.

Features

What it does

Market dashboardSwitch between stock and crypto views.
Charts and tradesReview charts, market activity and recorded trades.
Performance viewSee returns, asset trends and daily results by market.
Daily reviewLook back at the day’s trades.
RecordsReview observations and completed trades.
Manual choiceKeep control over how each holding is managed.
Risk settingsReview account connections and risk limits.

Safety

Controls for a personal experiment

No withdrawalsThere is no withdrawal function.
Risk limitsLimits are part of the experiment.
Duplicate-run checksAvoid running the same market task twice.
Confirm changesReview changes before applying them.
Emergency controlsKeep stop controls within reach.
Separate accountsKeep simulation and live-account views distinct.
Requirements and setup · Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Connection credentials are protected on the local PC.
