Airang — local information for families, on one screen

Airang is a free service I built for families in Korea, now covering 63 towns and districts across Gyeonggi, Seoul, Incheon and Chungnam. It brings school calendars and meals, libraries, kindergarten places, class fees, clinics, pharmacies, family benefits and events together. No sign-up is needed, and child profiles stay on the phone.

Why I built it

Parents had to check each source separately

School calendars, city events, library programmes, kindergarten places, after-school classes and local jobs were scattered across different sites. Airang brings them into one view.

Features

Eight menus

School calendarUpcoming school dates at a glance
Things to do with kidsCity events, festivals and library programmes
LibrariesReading-room availability and popular books
KindergartensPlaces, vacancies and school buses
After-school classesSearch over 3,400 providers and their reported fees
Class feesCompare typical fees by area and subject
Local jobs for parentsJobs at schools, kindergartens and childcare centres
High-school admissionsApplication dates and school options

Phone calendar reminders are also available before classes begin and end.

Fresh each morning

The information is refreshed each morning. Even if the service earns revenue, three principles stay the same: no collection of personal information, free core information and clearly labelled advertising.
Development log

How it got here

  • Early October 2026First idea — education information for parentsI started with education information for parents in Yongin, defining what would be useful before building the app.
  • Regular updatesFresh information each morningAdded daily updates for school dates, events, libraries, kindergartens and classes.
  • Web appA web app without installationPublished a web app that opens from a link. A child’s nickname and school stay on the user’s phone.
  • 2026.10.08Free pilot beginsOpened the service as a free pilot.
  • 2026.10.09 ~ 10Early feedbackThe first daily visitor counts were 89 and 90. Local community posts brought visitors, with the home screen, classes, kindergartens and libraries getting the most attention.
  • 2026.10.30Airang expands across GyeonggiRenamed the Yongin project Airang and expanded to other cities in Gyeonggi. Choose a town at airang.songgollab.co.kr.
  • 2026.11.02Seoul, Incheon and ChungnamAdded Seoul’s 25 districts, five areas in Incheon and Cheonan and Asan in Chungnam. Choose a region and then your town.
  • 2026.1131 in Gyeonggi; 63 in totalCoverage reached 31 areas in Gyeonggi and 63 across all four regions.
  • NextMore reasons to returnPlans include daily school meals, kindergarten application dates and a vote on the next feature.
