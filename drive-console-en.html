Why OBD

Why OBD?

At the repair shop you usually just describe the symptom. The mechanic guesses the cause and swaps a part. If that is not it, another part gets swapped… and the bill snowballs, while you still don't know the real cause.

1Live fault-code detectionPlug in an OBD dongle and Drive Console picks up the fault codes your car sends, even while you drive.
2Analysis · causeIt analyzes each code and shows what is likely wrong.
3Suspect parts · check stepsSuspect parts and the order to check them, all on one screen.
4Show your mechanicKnow the cause before you go, and show this screen to your mechanic.
Fault codes narrow down the cause; they are a guide. Have a mechanic confirm the final diagnosis.

Setup

Five steps

  1. Plug in the OBD donglePlug the dongle into the OBD port under the driver's side. When its light comes on, it's ready.
  2. Mount the tabletStick the mount on the dashboard and place the tablet in it.
  3. Install the appApply under "Testers wanted" below and we'll send you an install link. Open it on your phone, tap "Become a tester" → "Download", and it installs from Google Play.
  4. Getting started screenOnly one permission is required: allow Display over other apps. Optional: Microphone (voice control) and Location.
  5. Connect the dongleChoose your Bluetooth OBD dongle in Settings › Connection and the gauges start showing numbers. The engine must be on.

FAQ

FAQ

How do I install it?

Once you're registered as a tester, we'll send you an install link. Open it on your phone and tap "Become a tester" → "Download" to install from Google Play. No separate file is needed.

The dongle isn't found

Turn the engine on, turn on Bluetooth on the tablet, then choose the dongle again in Settings › Connection. If another app (e.g. Car Scanner) is connected to the dongle, close that app.

Some values are grey

Each car reports different values. Grey values are for reference only and have no normal range.

Can I use it while driving?

For safety, touch the screen only when stopped and use the voice assistant while driving. Video apps in split screen are hidden automatically while you drive.

Where can I leave a review?

Please leave a review on the Google Play page or send it through the contact page. We also add reviews here.

If you already use the 15-day trial from the blog, send us the device number from the lock screen on KakaoTalk when the trial ends.
